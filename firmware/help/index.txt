@page getting-started Getting started
This is the Open Graphing Calculator, an open-source graphing calculator with a computer algebra system. Press {on} to switch the calculator on. The home screen shows your calculations: type an expression and press {enter}. Results are exact by default:

    1/3+1/6
    ▶ 1/2
    √(8)
    ▶ 2*√(2)

## A first tour
- Arithmetic: `2+3*4` {enter}. Start a line with an operator to continue from the last answer (Ans).
- Store a value: `5` {→} {alpha} `A` {enter} gives `a:=5`. Use the name in later lines.
- Symbolic math: press {CAS}, choose `factor(` and type `x^2-1`: the result is `(x-1)*(x+1)`. {x} types `x`.
- Decimals: put a decimal point in a number, press {F5} (≈) instead of {enter}, use `approx(`, or set RESULT to APPROX in [[mode|settings]].
- Graph: press {F1} (graph) on the home screen, type `sin(x)` next to Y1, then {F5} (graph): the graph is a cell of the notebook; {enter} on it opens the full graph, where {F4} (trace) follows the curve. `sin(x-t)` moves: {F5} (play) on the selected cell.
- Apps: {apps} lists statistics, matrices, solvers, finance, base-n, units and more ([[apps]]).
- Every command: {shift} {apps} opens the [[catalog|catalog]]; {shift} {menu} on an entry explains it.
- The soft keys {F1} .. {F5} under the screen do what their labels at the bottom of the screen say ([[softkeys]]); {menu} lists them.
- Undo, copy and paste: {menu} {F1} undoes, {menu} {F3} copies, {menu} {F4} pastes ([[edit-bar]]).
- Find anything: {shift} {apps} and type a word (`det`, `undo`, `rpn`, `degree`): commands, apps and help pages.
- Settings ({shift} {home}): ANGLE switches to degrees, ENTRY to [[rpn|RPN]]; {◀} {▶} change a setting at once.

## Your work is kept
Variables, functions, the home history, Y= functions, the window and all settings are saved when the calculator switches off ({shift} {on}, or automatically after 5 minutes) and come back when you switch it on again, also after a battery change or a firmware update. See [[mem]].

@page editing Editing the entry line
The entry line works with whole tokens: `sin(` is one token and {⌫} removes it at once.

- {◀} {▶} move the cursor, {shift} {◀} / {shift} {▶} jump to the start / end.
- {⌫} deletes the token before the cursor (at the start of the line: the one under it).
- {shift} {⌫} switches between insert (default) and overwrite; OVR in the status bar.
- {clear} clears the line.
- {menu} {F1} undoes, {menu} {F2} redoes, {menu} {F3} / {menu} {F4} copy and paste: see [[edit-bar]].
- {→} stores: `expression→name` becomes `name:=expression`. {shift} {→} types `:=` directly.
- Menus ([[menu-cas|CAS]], [[menu-math|math]], [[catalog|catalog]], [[vars|vars]]) insert at the cursor; templates put the cursor where the argument goes, e.g. `solve(|,x)`.
- `°` ({shift} {,}, angle menu) marks degrees and `ʳ` radians, whatever the angle mode: `sin(30°)` is 1/2 in both modes.
- Relations for equations and tests are on {shift} {4} {5} {6} (`<` `=` `>`) and {shift} {1} {2} {3} (`≤` `≠` `≥`).
- `x(x+1)` with a one-letter name multiplies; longer names followed by `(` are function calls.
- Built-in names work in any case, so capitals typed with {alpha} {alpha} or on a computer keyboard are fine: `COS(x)`, `Sin(x)`, `LN(2)`, `SQRT(2)`, `NCR(5,2)` and the constants `PI`, `Pi`, `INF`, `ANS`. A function or variable you define with such a name (`COS(x):=...`, `PI:=3`) takes precedence. `e` and `i` are the only spellings of Euler's number and the imaginary unit: `E` and `I` are ordinary variables, like every other name, since variables are case-sensitive (`X` and `x` differ).

@page help Using help
Help pages explain screens, menus and every command.

- {shift} {menu} (help) opens the page for the current screen, or for the highlighted entry when a menu or the catalog is open.
- {▲} {▼} scroll one line, {+} and {−} (or {F2} {F1}) scroll a page.
- {◀} {▶} highlight the previous / next link, {enter} opens it.
- {⌫} or {F5} (back) goes back to the previous page; {clear} or {F4} (exit) closes help.
- {F3} (index) or {shift} {menu} inside help opens the [[index|help index]].
- Touch: drag to scroll, tap a link to open it.

Command pages show the syntax, a description, examples (the line after ▶ is the result) and related commands.

@page touch Touch gestures
The touch screen is optional: everything also works with the keys. A tap also ends a one-shot {shift} or {alpha}.

- Soft keys: tap a label in the bar at the bottom of the screen to press that key (after {shift}: its second function).

- Scrolling: on every screen and menu that scrolls, drag to scroll; the content follows your finger (drag up to see what is below, left to see the columns on the right), and the selection moves along when it would leave the screen. A drag never chooses or runs anything.
- Menus: tap an entry to choose it; drag up or down to scroll.
- Home screen: tap a history entry to select it, tap it again to paste it into the entry line; tap the entry line to move the cursor; tap empty space to deselect; drag down to look back through the history (any key returns to the entry line).
- Y=: tap the colour swatch to change the colour, the `=` to switch the function on or off, the expression to edit it.
- Graph: drag to pan, pinch with two fingers to zoom, tap to trace the nearest function at that x.
- Swipe (a quick, straight flick): in help, swipe right to go back and left for the next page; in Math Quest, swipe between rooms, worlds and book or shop pages, and flick celebrations away. A slow drag still scrolls.
- Table: tap a cell to select it, drag to scroll.
- Settings: tap an option to select it.
- WINDOW, TBLSET and the forms of the statistics, solver and finance apps: tap a field to edit it.
- List editor, spreadsheet and matrix grids: tap a cell to select it; drag in any direction to scroll.
- Help: drag to scroll, tap a link.
- Messages: tap to close.

@page power Power and battery
- {on} switches the calculator on. {shift} {on} switches it off.
- After 5 minutes without a key press or touch it switches itself off (not while a computer uses its USB connection, see [[usb]]).
- Switching off saves your session (see [[mem]]); switching on restores it, so you continue where you stopped.
- The battery symbol in the status bar shows the charge of the 4 AAA cells (USB when powered by USB). It turns red when the cells are low (below 1.0 V per cell); replace them soon. Below 0.9 V per cell the calculator saves the session and switches off.
- The calculator runs from USB-C power too; unplugging USB with cells fitted does not interrupt it.
- {shift} {▲} / {shift} {▼} change the brightness; LIGHT in [[mode|settings]] sets it too. A darker screen makes the cells last longer.

@page errors Error messages
Errors appear in red in the history. The entry is kept, press {shift} {enter} (entry) to recall and fix it.

- ERR:SYNTAX: the input cannot be read (missing parenthesis, two operators in a row, ...).
- ERR:DOMAIN: a value outside the domain, e.g. division by zero or `ln(0)`.
- ERR:UNDEFINED: an unknown name or function.
- ERR:MEMORY: the computation ran out of memory or was nested too deeply.
- ERR:BREAK: stopped with {on}.
- ERR:UNSUPPORTED: valid input that the CAS cannot handle yet.
- ERR:NONREAL ANS: a complex result while COMPLEX is REAL in [[mode-complex|settings]].
