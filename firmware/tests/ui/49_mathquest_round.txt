# Sparkle Town: Hop Along's rounding teaches the method (the user: "teach rounding as opposed to just picking the
# closer side"; "show the number, but not on the scale"). The number is on its card, never on the line. The help
# fades: (0) the line shows the two round numbers and the middle; (1) she finds the two round numbers first (three
# pairs to pick); (2) the numbers only. After a right hop the line shows where the number really sits and why it
# goes that way ("47 is past the middle: up to 50!", "45 is on the middle: the middle goes up").
requirecfg CONTENT_QSPI
fs insert 64
fs format
module library extra
module install /CALC/MODULES/{mod:mathquest}
key CLEAR
app mathquest
mq seed 3
mq fun off
mq scene off
mq new Mia 3
mq engine stop
mq grant bosses
# (0) the most help: both tens and the middle, the number on its card only
mq roundhelp 0
mq skill 15 0
expect state mq_mech "Hop Along"
expect statecontains mq_round "0 1"
mq step 10
expect text the middle
screenshot mq_round_help0.png
mq correct
mq step 2
expect text the middle
screenshot mq_round_reveal.png
mq step 60
# (1) she finds the two tens first: three pairs, no go button yet; a wrong pair wobbles with a hint
mq roundhelp 1
mq skill 15 0
expect statecontains mq_round "1 0"
mq step 10
expect text Which two tens is it between?
screenshot mq_round_help1.png
mq correct
expect state mq_moment cheer
mq step 60
# (2) the numbers only: no line, the two round numbers on the pads
mq roundhelp 2
mq skill 15 1
expect statecontains mq_round "2 1"
mq step 10
screenshot mq_round_help2.png
mq correct
mq step 2
screenshot mq_round_help2_reveal.png
mq step 60
# halfway: the middle rounds up, shown
mq roundhelp 0
mq skill 15 3
mq step 10
screenshot mq_round_half.png
mq correct
mq step 2
screenshot mq_round_half_reveal.png
mq roundhelp -1
